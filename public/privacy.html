AirScribe privacy policy

This page is the same on every AirScribe server.

AirScribe records radio traffic picked up by the radios of whoever runs this server, writes it out as text, and shows it to the people they give accounts to. It runs on their own computer. It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Your account

  • Your username, your role (viewer or admin), when the account was made and when it last signed in.
  • Your password, kept only as a one-way hash (scrypt). Nobody can read it back, the server's owner included.
  • If you set up two-step verification: your passkeys' public keys, your authenticator app's secret, your recovery codes as hashes, and browsers you asked to be remembered (for 30 days).
  • If you fill in Amateur radio: your callsign and beacon text.

Signing in with Google, GitHub, Discord or Facebook

If you link one of these accounts, AirScribe asks it only who you are. From Google that's the basic sign-in scopes, openid email profile. It keeps the account's ID, name and e-mail address. These are used for one thing: recognising you when you sign in with that account. AirScribe never matches people by e-mail address, never sends you e-mail, and never shares any of this. Unlinking the account, or deleting yours, deletes what was kept. AirScribe doesn't ask for, and can't reach, your mail, contacts, files or anything else in that account.

Cookies and addresses

  • Cookies only to keep you signed in (12 hours after you last use it), finish a two-step sign-in, and remember a browser you chose to. No other cookies, and none from anyone else.
  • The address you connect from is held in memory for 15 minutes to limit wrong passwords, then forgotten.

The radio traffic

Recordings, transcripts and what's worked out from them (addresses, plates, units mentioned) come from what was said over the air, not from you. They're kept on this server for the time its owner sets, then deleted. The transcription and the language model run on servers its owner chooses. Some of it can be published to visitors who aren't signed in, after a delay, if the owner turns that on.

Who else sees anything

Nothing about you leaves this server, except:

  • the sign-in provider, while you sign in with it;
  • the map server this AirScribe uses, which sees the map tiles your browser asks for.

Your data

To see, change or delete what's kept about you, ask whoever runs this server (the person who gave you your account). An admin can delete an account, and that deletes its sign-ins, linked accounts, two-step and profile. You can change your password, unlink accounts and sign out everywhere yourself, under your name in the header.
