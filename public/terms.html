AirScribe terms of use

This page is the same on every AirScribe server.

  • Accounts are by invitation. Whoever runs this server decides who has one, and can change a role, turn an account off or delete it at any time.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Don't share your password or let others use your account.
  • Transcripts can be wrong. Speech recognition mishears radio, and a language model can turn a misheard word into a confident wrong one. Play the recording before relying on anything, and never use AirScribe in place of calling for help or official information.
  • What you hear here is what was said on the air. Follow the laws where you are on listening to radio and on using or repeating what you hear, especially about people named in it.
  • No guarantee. AirScribe is provided as it is, and may be down, change or stop at any time.

What's kept about you is in the privacy policy.
