AirScribe records radio traffic picked up by our radios (public safety, public works and amateur radio), writes it out as text with speech recognition, and may use a language model to pick out details such as addresses, units and plates. What you see is what was said on the air, as a machine heard it.

It can be wrong. Speech recognition mishears radio, and a language model can turn a misheard word into a confident wrong address, plate or name. Play the recording before relying on anything.
